🐉 Dragon's Hollow

Fight · Explore · Team Up

The dragon Emberoth has stirred beneath the Hollow. Build your hero, gather your courage (and maybe a co-pilot), and drive the shadow back before it swallows the woods.

How to Play

Player 1 (keyboard)
Move: W A S D
Attack: F   Special: G
Player 2 (keyboard)
Move: Arrow Keys
Attack: K   Special: L
Touch
Left stick to move
Buttons to Attack / Special

Clear each room of enemies to unlock the way forward. In the Dungeon Depths, two heroes must each stand on a glowing rune plate at the same time to open the teamwork gate! If a hero falls, their partner can revive them by standing close and holding Attack.

Defeated enemies drop XP and sometimes gear! Tap the ⭐ Skill Tree button (top-right, next to pause) any time to spend skill points and see equipped weapons, armor, and trinkets you've found.
